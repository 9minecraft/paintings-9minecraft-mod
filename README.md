## About this mod

Paintings ++ 9minecraft Mod gives you full control over which artwork goes on your walls. Instead of breaking and placing a painting over and over until you get the right picture, this mod opens a simple menu where you can pick exactly what you want. It is perfect for decorating houses, building museums, or setting up a custom art gallery. Features: Painting Menu: Opens a special screen when you place a painting so you can pick the exact picture you want.
